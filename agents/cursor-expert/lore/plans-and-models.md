# Plans and models

Scope: individual and business plans, what each plan unlocks, how usage is billed, and which
models Cursor offers.

**Never quote a price, limit, or model from memory.** Recheck https://cursor.com/pricing (The X
Subscription), https://cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing.md (Cursor plans, pools, model rates), and
https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/pricing.md. Since 2026-10-12 these pages describe two
different plan families; say which one a fact belongs to.

## Plan-gated features seen in the docs

Cursor plan family, checked 2026-10-14 (documented):

- **Cloud Agents, Projects, Cursor for iOS, Remote Control, Origin**: paid plans (Start, Pro, Pro+,
  Ultra, Teams, Enterprise). Not Hobby. Projects and cloud agents also need Privacy Mode, not
  Privacy Mode (Legacy).
- **Hobby (free)**: Agent, Chat, Tab with the Auto model; limited usage; monthly Tab allowance.
- **Start (India)**: Cursor Models only; no Other Models, on-demand, Bugbot, Auto, Automations, or
  SDK.
- **Cursor Router** (Auto with Balance and Intelligence): Teams and Enterprise now; individual
  plans "a few months after launch".
- **Shared transcripts**: Teams and Enterprise. **Shared canvases**: paid plans on a team.
- **Team marketplaces, Team Rules, Team MCP servers**: Teams (1 marketplace) and Enterprise.
- **Jira integration, Rollouts, CI autofix**: Teams and Enterprise (CI autofix: Teams only for
  now).
- **Enterprise only**: MCP Allowlist, model access control, SCIM, audit logs, service accounts,
  Team Pools, team hooks, Cursor Blame, OpenTelemetry Export, Admin/Analytics APIs, Bugbot API
  ([admin controls](admin-and-enterprise-controls.md)).
- **Grok Bot**: every paid individual plan and every self-serve Teams seat.

The X Subscription family gates differently: Plus has Grok models only and no cloud agents;
Premium ($30) and up add cloud agents, automations, Origin, Bugbot, third-party models, and Grok
Bot ([plans and pricing](plans-and-pricing.md)).
